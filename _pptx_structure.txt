WHY STORE VISIT LAG MATTERS FOR TARGET CPA BIDDING
---
MAX CONVERSIONS (SYSTEM DEFAULT)
---
Feedback speed: NOT CRITICAL
---
Bids to maximize total conversions within budget. Doesn't target a specific cost threshold — so it doesn't need fast conversion feedback. It works fine with lagged store visits because it's not adjusting bids per-auction against a CPA target.
---
TARGET CPA (TEST)
---
Feedback speed: CRITICAL
---
Actively adjusts bids per-auction against a cost target. Needs timely conversion signals to know whether recent auctions hit the target. When store visits report days late, it can't attribute them to the auctions that caused them — so it under-credits store-visit-heavy impressions (Maps, local inventory) and over-indexes on fast-reporting signals (directions, menu views).
---
RESULT
Target CPA optimizes around the store-visit goal, not toward it. The incremental conversions are softer local actions (directions, menu views) that don't translate to foot traffic.
